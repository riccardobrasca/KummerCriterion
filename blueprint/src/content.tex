\begin{abstract}
This document gives a proof of \textbf{Kummer's criterion}. It develops the
class-number, Bernoulli, and cyclotomic-unit ingredients needed to prove the
final equivalence
\[
  p \text{ is regular}
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \forall k,\ 1 \le k,\ 2k \le p - 3,\quad
  p \nmid (B_{2k})_{\mathrm{num}}
\]
for every odd prime $p$.

The first part of the argument proves the classical formula for the relative
class number
\[
  h^- = \frac{h\bigl(\Q(\zeta_p)\bigr)}{h\bigl(\Q(\zeta_p)^+\bigr)}.
\]
The second part uses the minus class-number criterion, the $p$-adic logarithm
determinant of real cyclotomic units, and the prime-conductor cyclotomic-unit
index theorem to compare the plus and minus class-number divisibilities. The
final proof chapter assembles these results into Kummer's criterion.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Outline of the argument.}

We begin with the CM extension
\(
  \Q(\zeta_p) / \Q(\zeta_p)^+
\)
and prove that the class number of the maximal real subfield divides the full
class number. This allows one to define the relative class number $h^-$
honestly by division; see \cref{ch:cm}.

Next we introduce Dirichlet characters modulo $p$ and the associated
generalised Bernoulli numbers $B_{n,\chi}$; see
\cref{ch:characters} and \cref{ch:bernoulli}. The basic properties needed later are the
vanishing of $B_{1,\chi}$ for non-trivial even characters, the $p$-integrality
of the classical Bernoulli numbers below the boundary $p-1$, and the
description of the boundary term attached to $\omega^{-1}$.

In \cref{ch:lvalue-negative} we compute the odd special values
\(
  L(0,\chi)
\)
and
\(
  L(1,\chi)
\)
in terms of generalised Bernoulli numbers and Gauss sums.

Finally, in \cref{ch:hminus-formula}, we factor the Dedekind zeta functions of
\(\Q(\zeta_p)\) and \(\Q(\zeta_p)^+\), compare their residues at $s=1$, and
obtain the analytic class number formula
\[
  h^- = 2p \prod_{\chi\text{ odd}}
  \left(-\frac12 B_{1,\chi^{-1}}\right).
\]

The cyclotomic-unit chapter then introduces the real cyclotomic units in
$\Q(\zeta_p)^+$, the subgroups they generate, exact $p$-saturation, and the
Kummer logarithm determinant. Its main output is that non-divisibility of all
Bernoulli numerators in Kummer's range implies
\[
  p \nmid [(\mathcal O_{\Q(\zeta_p)^+})^\times : C^+].
\]

The final proof chapter combines this index non-divisibility with the
prime-conductor index theorem
\[
  p \mid [(\mathcal O_{\Q(\zeta_p)^+})^\times : C^+]
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  p \mid h^+
\]
and with the minus class-number criterion. This proves
$p \mid h \Longleftrightarrow p$ divides one of the relevant Bernoulli
numerators, and hence Kummer's criterion.

The next chapter applies Kummer's criterion and the full divided Kummer
congruence to Carlitz's construction.  It proves that for every finite set of
candidate primes there is an irregular prime outside the set, and therefore
that there are infinitely many irregular primes.

The final chapter records the reflection route that was developed but not used
in the main proof.  That route tries to
obtain the plus-to-minus implication from the reflection theorem rather than
from cyclotomic-unit index computations.
\end{abstract}
